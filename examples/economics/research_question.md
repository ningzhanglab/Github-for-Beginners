# Toy Economics Study — Research Question

**Question:** How did local unemployment rates and average hourly wages change across four fictional counties between 2024 and 2025?

## Motivation

Economic research often combines a written research question, a small panel dataset, a set of variable definitions, a table of results, and a research log. This example shows why preserving their history in GitHub is helpful when a teammate or AI agent changes them.

## What we plan to compare

- County-level changes in the unemployment rate, measured in percentage points.
- County-level wage growth, measured as the percent change in nominal average hourly wages.

## Limitations

- All data are **synthetic and entirely invented**.
- The four counties are fictional. No real source supports these values.
- Eight county-year rows are far too little for serious empirical analysis.
- A descriptive correlation cannot establish causality.
- We intentionally do not claim a regression estimate, statistical significance, or a policy effect.

This is a teaching exercise for project management, not an economics result.
