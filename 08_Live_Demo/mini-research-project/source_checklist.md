# Source-Checking Checklist — Demonstration

This checklist is an **illustrative change proposed from a separate branch** as part of [teaching Issue #1](https://github.com/ningzhanglab/Github-for-Beginners/issues/1).

When reviewing a paper or other source, record:

- [ ] **Title and author:** Is the reference identifiable?
- [ ] **Publication date:** When was it published or updated?
- [ ] **Publisher or venue:** Where was it released? Is it appropriate to the research question?
- [ ] **Persistent link:** Is a DOI, official webpage, or stable citation available?
- [ ] **Relevance:** Which claim in the reading summary is this source meant to support?
- [ ] **Evidence:** Is the finding supported by the source as written?
- [ ] **Limitations:** Are uncertainty and missing data clearly labeled?
- [ ] **Independent verification:** Did a human open or check the referenced source?

## Example status labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified | A reviewer checked the source and its relevant claim |
| Provisional | Plausible, but not independently checked |
| Missing | Required source information is unavailable |

**Important:** The checklist is a process aid, not evidence that any sources were actually verified.
