# Example Research Plan — v2 (reviewed demonstration)

**Project:** Fictional Student Reading Summary

## Question
How can two students reliably prepare and review a short literature summary?

## Planned workflow
1. Alex outlines the paper-selection criteria.
2. Sam reads the draft and suggests clarifications.
3. The team prepares a final summary.
4. Before the final version is accepted, the team reviews the [source-checking checklist](source_checklist.md).

## Source-quality rule
A written checklist now exists, but **no real papers or claims have been verified** as part of this example. A human reviewer remains responsible for reviewing sources.

## Deadline
Example milestone: finish an initial draft by the end of the week.

## Important scope note
